Tips for terms

Separate terms with commas, semicolons or new lines. A term can be several words, such as urban cycling: an article with more of them scores higher, and they don't have to stand together. Related forms are found too (cycling also finds cyclist), and a translation is simply another term. Specific beats general: health on its own matches almost everything. How matching works

Basic relevance

{{ switch(name="basic_scoring_enabled", id="basic_scoring_enabled", label="Score every article by your terms", checked=s.basic_scoring_enabled) }}

Runs on everything that arrives, from the title and the first 300 characters, with no API key and no cost. It is a weaker signal than AI scoring: it matches words, so it cannot tell that a report from the Tour de France is about cycling unless one of your terms appears in it. Measured against what people actually read, it ranks clearly better than plain date order and clearly worse than the AI score.
