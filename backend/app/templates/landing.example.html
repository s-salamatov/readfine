An RSS reader that sorts your feeds
before you do.

Tell it what you're into and the best articles come first.
The same story from five sites shows up as one.

Subscribe to feeds, scrape sites that don't have one, or save any link to read later. Filters label or clear out what you tell them to, and with your own AI key it can summarize articles and brief you on what you missed. It's open source: use it here for free, or run it on your own server.

How it works

  1. {{ badge(text="STEP 1", variant="secondary") }}

    Pick your topics

    Tell it a few things you follow. Every article gets scored against them, no AI key needed.

  2. {{ badge(text="STEP 2", variant="secondary") }}

    Add your sources

    Start with a few well-known feeds, bring your subscriptions over as OPML, or paste any site's address.

  3. {{ badge(text="STEP 3", variant="secondary") }}

    Best first

    New articles arrive on their own, sorted by what you care about, with a story from several sites folded into one row.

  4. {{ badge(text="STEP 4", variant="secondary") }}

    Read & ask

    Read the clean view, and with your own AI key, get a summary or chat about the article.

Open source, and yours to run

{% call card(title="Self-host it", heading="h3") -%}

Readfine is free and AGPL licensed, so you can read every line of it. Run it on your own server with Docker and your feeds, articles and keys never leave the machine. Or sign up here and let us host it for you.

{%- endcall %} {% call card(title="Run the AI locally too", heading="h3") -%}

The AI part doesn't have to be somebody's cloud either. Point Readfine at anything that speaks the OpenAI API: Ollama or llama.cpp on your own machine, vLLM, a LiteLLM proxy, a gateway like OpenRouter. A local server needs no key at all and nothing leaves your network.
